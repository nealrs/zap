## Level-by-Level Details

### Level 1: The Void
**Purpose**: Learn basic controls
- Largest bubbles (1.2 size) - easiest to click
- Lowest gravity - slowest movement
- Respawning enabled - infinite practice
- **Time**: 40 seconds is generous
- **Pops**: 12 out of 15 available (easy)

**Strategy**: Just click bubbles. Get comfortable with the controls.

---

### Level 2: Floating Garden  
**Purpose**: First moving hazard introduction
- Comet moves randomly through the field
- Attracts bubbles in a trailing wake (up to 20)
- 22 bubbles, need 18 pops
- Comet moves at slow, steady pace
- No respawning

**Strategy**: Track the comet's movement. Click bubbles before they get caught in the trail.

---

### Level 3: Event Horizon
**Purpose**: Teleporting hazard introduction
- Single wormhole at side position
- Pulls bubbles in and teleports them randomly
- 24 bubbles, need 20 pops
- Wormhole strength is moderate (r=7, s=0.025)
- No respawning

**Strategy**: Notice how the wormhole pulls and teleports bubbles. Time your clicks carefully.

---

### Level 4: Dual Vortex
**Purpose**: Multiple hazards of same type
- Two wormholes create opposing pull forces
- Respawning disabled - manage your shots!
- 30 bubbles, need 25 pops
- Higher gravity (0.0002)

**Strategy**: Bubbles get pulled by wormholes. Click them before they get trapped!

---

### Level 5: The Repulsor Core
**Purpose**: Learn repulsor physics
- Repulsor at center pushes everything away
- Must hunt the bubbles as they flee outward
- 28 bubbles, need 30 pops
- Respawning enabled to reach target

**Strategy**: Anticipate where bubbles flee. Chase them to the edges.

---

### Level 6: Bouncing Chaos
**Purpose**: Learn bumper physics  
- Bumper at center reflects bubbles back
- Creates chaotic bouncing patterns
- 26 bubbles, need 28 pops
- Respawning enabled to reach target

**Strategy**: Watch the bounce patterns. The center bumper creates wild trajectories.

---

### Level 7: Wormhole Madness
**Purpose**: Introduce time bonuses
- Wormhole + time bonus special bubbles
- 32 bubbles, need 35 pops (need the bonuses!)
- Only 50 seconds - must use time boosts
- No respawning

**Strategy**: Prioritize special bubbles for time extensions.

---

### Level 8: Repulsor Onslaught
**Purpose**: Use point bonuses
- Repulsor at center with point bonus special bubbles
- 30 bubbles, need 40 pops
- Respawning enabled (helps reach 40 pop requirement)
- Point bonuses count as +3 pops each

**Strategy**: Grab point bonuses to accelerate progress. Chase fleeing bubbles.
- No special bubble spam (0.12 spawn rate)

**Strategy**: Chase the fleeing bubbles. Point bonuses give progress.

---

### Level 9: Vortex Eruption
**Purpose**: Master slow-motion
- Wormhole + slow-motion special bubbles
- 38 bubbles, need 42 pops
- Slow-motion is crucial for handling speed
- 58 seconds total

**Strategy**: Use slow-motion to line up difficult clicks near wormhole.

---

### Level 10: Explosion Protocol (FINAL)
**Purpose**: Ultimate challenge
- Repulsor + multipop (area explosion)
- 40 bubbles, need 45 pops
- Highest difficulty (0.75 pops/sec)
- Must use multipos strategically
- 60 seconds = maximum time allowed

**Strategy**: Use multipop to eliminate clusters. Chain reactions!

## Hazard Configurations

### Wormhole
- Pulls bubbles toward center
- Teleports if very close
- Strength: 0.025 → 0.050
- Radius: 6 → 7

### Repulsor  
- Pushes bubbles away
- Strength increases with difficulty
- Strength: 0.045 → 0.065
- Radius: 8 → 10

### Bumper
- Reflects bubbles
- Only appears once (Level 6)
- Single hazard
- Strength: 0.05

## Recommended Play Order

**First Time Players**:
1. Level 1-3: Tutorials (15-20 min)
2. Level 4-6: Medium (15-20 min)
3. Level 7-10: Hard (20-25 min)

**Challenge Route**:
- Try going straight to Level 5 after Level 1
- Level 10 is the true final boss

**Practice Route**:
- Replay levels to build speed
- Levels 1-3 for warmup
- Levels 7-10 for skill challenge
